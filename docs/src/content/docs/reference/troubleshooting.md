# Troubleshooting & FAQ

Here are solutions to the most common setup and execution issues.

---

## 1. "OAuth redirect URI does not match"

### Cause:
The OAuth Callback URL generated by n8n does not match the URI configured in the Meta Developer App dashboard.

### Fix:
1. In n8n, check the **Instagram OAuth2 API** credential modal and copy the exact **OAuth Callback URL** (e.g. `https://n8n.example.com/rest/oauth2-credential/callback`).
2. In Meta App Dashboard, go to **Instagram Platform** > **API setup with Instagram login** > **3. Set up Instagram business login** > **Business login settings**.
3. Under **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs**, paste the exact URL and click **Save Changes**.

---

## 2. "Invalid OAuth access token / User has not authorized application"

### Cause:
Your Meta Developer App is in **Development Mode**, and the Instagram account attempting to authenticate has not been added as an Instagram Tester or has not accepted the invite.

### Fix:
1. Go to **App Roles** > **Roles** > **Instagram Testers** in Meta Dashboard.
2. Add the Instagram username.
3. On Instagram (web or mobile), navigate to **Settings & Privacy** > **Website Permissions** > **Apps and Websites** > **Tester Invites**, and click **Accept**.

---

## 3. "Media upload timed out / Container status failed"

### Cause:
Instagram requires media files to be publicly accessible and in an accepted format. If the file is inaccessible or transcoding takes longer than the timeout period, container creation fails.

### Fix:
- Ensure the image/video URL is publicly accessible over HTTPS without basic auth or firewalls.
- Video recommendations: MP4 format, H.264 video codec, AAC audio codec, maximum 1080p resolution.
- Photos: Standard JPEG format, max file size 8 MB.

---

## 4. "Application does not have permission for this action" (#200 / #10)

### Cause:
The authenticated token lacks the specific scope needed for the operation (for example, trying to send a DM without `instagram_business_manage_messages`).

### Fix:
- Check your **Instagram OAuth2 API** credential scope configuration in n8n.
- Re-authenticate by clicking **Connect my account** to grant the missing permission.
